**Sandbox Notes** ("this site", https://sandbox-notes.github.io/) respects your privacy. This page explains what information is handled and how.

## 1. Information we collect

This site has no accounts or comments and does not ask visitors to submit personal information. While you browse, the following may be generated or collected automatically:

- Server logs: IP address, browser type, time of visit, pages viewed (recorded by the hosting provider)
- Cookies and similar technologies, where used for advertising and traffic statistics

## 2. How we use it

- To operate and secure the site
- To understand traffic and improve content
- To serve and measure advertising

## 3. Advertising and cookies

This site may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites.

- How Google uses cookies in advertising: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- Manage or turn off personalized ads: <https://adssettings.google.com>
- Opt out of third-party vendors' personalized ads: <https://www.aboutads.info/choices/>

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may limit some functionality.

## 4. Analytics

This site currently does not use a separate analytics tool. If one is added, this section will name the tool and the data it collects.

## 5. Sharing and processors

We do not sell visitor information. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and advertising providers may process information as needed to deliver their services, under their own policies.

## 6. Retention

Logs and similar data are retained according to the policies of the hosting and advertising providers. This site does not keep its own database of personal information.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal information. Although this site does not hold such data itself, we will review any request and respond. Visitors in the European Economic Area have rights under the GDPR.

## 8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 14 (or the applicable age in your region) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 9. External links

Posts may link to external sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

## 10. About security content

This site documents malware analysis. It never provides malware samples; only hashes and links to public analyses are published. Indicators of compromise are defanged (for example `[.]`) to prevent accidental use.

This policy may change with the law or the service. Updates will be posted here with a new date.
